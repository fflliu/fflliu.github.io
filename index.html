Fan Liu

Ph.D. student/Georgia Tech ↗/Atlanta, GA

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Mathematics at Georgia Tech, advised by Dr. Wei Zhu. I received my B.S. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Tianjin University.

I am interested in generative models, both in improving their performance and in understanding their underlying mechanisms through theory. I am also interested in applying machine learning tools to scientific problems.

My interests initially focused exclusively on machine learning theory. I am now beginning to explore computational work, with the aim of connecting theory more closely with practice.
